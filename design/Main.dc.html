Yoink the to-dos straight out of your meeting notes.
Why it works
It is a verb your users will actually say. “Yoink it into my list” describes the whole product in two words: grab the action item, drop it in the list. Short, unforgettable, and it makes a dull chore sound like a small win.
The tradeoff
It is slang, so it skews young and informal. If you ever pitch to an enterprise buyer or a procurement team, the name carries no gravity at all. Strongest if your audience is individuals and small teams.
